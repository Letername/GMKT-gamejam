# Portfolio art for the GOOD ad cut

The good cut of the in-game Letername ad shows the developer's real projects.
Drop images in this folder and the ad picks them up automatically — no code
change needed.

## Naming

    NN_projectname_label.png

The `NN` prefix sets the order (`01`, `02`, `03`…). Everything after it is
ignored by the loader and is just for you.

## What to drop in

Per project, 2–4 images works best for a 30-second cut:

- **A logo or icon** — square, ideally 512x512 or larger.
- **1–3 screenshots** of the app actually doing its thing. Landscape 16:9 is
  ideal; the ad letterboxes anything else rather than cropping it.

PNG or JPG. Anything at least 512px on its long edge is fine.
