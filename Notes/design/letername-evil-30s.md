# Letername ClearHome — 30-second production script

> Archived concept. The produced live-action creative is now documented in
> `letername-scan-and-surrender.md` and uses the player's final recorded lines.

This replaces the earlier "we take all your money" slideshow concept. The ad is
now a polished product launch for **ClearHome**, a financial-wellness service
that removes money stress by cataloguing and repossessing everything a customer
owns.

The joke is still played completely straight. The narrator believes this is a
helpful, premium service. The visuals use calm product-design language,
measurable outcomes, immaculate cards, and reassuring motion. They never admit
that Bob's situation is horrifying.

Three performance rules:

1. **Never wink.** Read every claim like a real financial-services commercial.
2. **Treat the metrics as good news.** Zero accounts and one floor are successful outcomes.
3. **Let the exact product language land.** "Asset Concierge" and "removes the ownership" are the punchlines.

position as the animation clock. When a WAV is absent, the player uses the
durations above, which total exactly 30 seconds.

## Recording

- Record one file per ID: `evil_01.wav` through `evil_08.wav`.
- Use 48 kHz, mono, 16-bit WAV.
- Leave roughly one second of silence at the head and tail; do not trim tightly.
- Record the complete ad in one sitting so the commercial tone stays consistent.
- Do a second pass about 10% slower.
- Put the files directly in `assets/ads/`; the player detects them without a code change.
- Optional alternate filenames remain supported for beats 03, 05, and 07.
