# Letername Scan & Surrender — live-action production script

This is the produced rewarded-ad creative. It replaces the archived ClearHome
concept. The player's phone footage is treated as an intentional live asset
scanner, with Letername interface graphics and the team-made IRS robot composited
over it. The first four portrait clips use scanner side panels; the landscape
clips open into full-screen appraisal and consolidation beats.

The performance stays completely sincere. The narrator reads every line as a
premium technology commercial and never acknowledges the threat in the words.

Official Letername Technologies motto:

> **WE DON'T WANT JUST SOME OF YOUR MONEY. WE WANT ALL OF IT.**

The animation clock follows each WAV's real playback position. If a WAV is
missing, the original 30-second fallback durations remain available. The skip
button unlocks after 15 seconds of actual viewing, even when the recorded read
runs longer than the fallback cut.

## Audio

- The player's nine recordings are level-matched around -17 LUFS. `evil_09` was
  raised from -32.3 LUFS to -17.8 LUFS with peak limiting at -1.1 dBFS.
- `Advertime` by Rafael Krux provides the deliberately sincere commercial bed.
- The music is CC0 and its provenance is recorded in `assets/ads/MUSIC-LICENSE.md`.
- The music master is normalized below the voice and both use the dedicated
  `AdAudio` bus.
