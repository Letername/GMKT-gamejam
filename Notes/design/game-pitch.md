# Tax Season Siege — Game Pitch

## One-sentence hook

Gather from an ever-depleting forest, fortify Bob's yard, and survive escalating IRS raids long enough to conquer the cave and build its forbidden crystal defenses.

## Player fantasy

The player is an improvised survivalist turning household desperation into an increasingly ridiculous defensive machine. Each round asks them to balance immediate safety against longer expeditions for the rare resources that unlock stronger options.

## Core loop

1. Gather wood and stone during a timed preparation phase.
2. Place walls and sentries on the outlined yard tiles.
3. Spend, sell, heal, and restock at the arms dealer.
4. Defend the house and its five valuables from an IRS wave.
5. Push deeper into the forest and cave for iron, powder, gold, permanent upgrades, and crystals.
6. Survive as many increasingly difficult rounds as possible.

## Experience goals

- Immediately readable and funny, with the team's handmade pixel art always foregrounded.
- Approachable during the first two rounds, then steadily demanding rather than punishing.
- Meaningful tradeoffs between gathering, building, ammunition, exploration, and starting a wave early.
- Short-session satisfaction with an endless high-score reason to return.

## Win and loss

The cave boss and crystal sentry form the run's major mastery milestone. The endless score continues beyond that milestone. The run ends when the IRS steals Bob's prized KFZ Air Fryer Console.

## Scope guardrails

- Preserve the team's original character, environment, prop, and interface artwork.
- Prefer deepening the gather-build-defend loop over adding unrelated modes.
- Every new system must communicate its purpose before it adds difficulty.
- Portfolio builds must launch quickly, save settings reliably, and remain playable without developer controls.
